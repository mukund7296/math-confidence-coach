# Dividing fractions
keywords: fractions fraction division divide dividing reciprocal invert
Source: written by the candidate for this prototype (original text, no copyright issues).

Key idea: a ÷ b asks "how many groups of b fit into a?". For 3/4 ÷ 1/8: how many eighths fit in three quarters? 3/4 = 6/8, so 6 eighths fit: the answer is 6.
Why "multiply by the reciprocal" works: dividing by 1/8 means counting eighths; there are 8 eighths in every whole, so dividing by 1/8 is the same as multiplying by 8, the reciprocal of 1/8. For 2/3 the reciprocal is 3/2: there are 3/2 "two-thirds" in each whole.
Worked example: 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.
Common errors: dividing numerators and denominators separately without noticing it works only sometimes; flipping the wrong fraction; believing division always makes the number smaller (false when dividing by a number below 1).
